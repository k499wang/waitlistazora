# Calm Reset — Image Ad Test Plan (Burned Out / Focus-Drained Persona)

Funnel: `calm-reset` (`lib/funnels/registry.ts`), branch: `general_feel` →
drained / scattered (wellness & focus goals).

**Persona:** The Burned-Out / Focus-Drained — running on empty, scattered
attention, "tried everything," skeptical of generic wellness advice.

**Core message (the reframe):** It's not a discipline/focus problem — it's a
nervous system stuck in "on" mode that never got the "all clear" signal. This
mirrors the funnel's existing "open loop" / failure-reframe screens, so the ad
message and the quiz experience stay consistent.

**Channel:** Meta (FB/IG feed), cold traffic → click sends to `/f/calm-reset`.

**Format constraint:** No app screenshots/mockups for this round. Native/organic
look (Notes app, fake tweet, candid photo) or AI-generated photo/illustration +
text overlay.

---

## Core copy hooks (image headline / overlay text)

**Pain-only (problem-aware, no reframe):**
1. "running on empty and it's only tuesday"
2. "i used to have so much energy. now i can't focus through a netflix episode"
3. "tired all day, wired all night"
4. "everyone says 'just rest' like i haven't tried"

**The reframe (nervous-system / dopamine-baseline angle):**
5. "you don't have a focus problem. you have a nervous system stuck in 'on' mode."
6. "burnout isn't a personality flaw. it's a stress response that never got the 'all clear' signal."
7. "'can't focus' and 'always tired' come from the same place — a nervous system that never fully switches off."
8. "it's not that you're lazy. your body forgot how to turn off."

**Quiz teaser:**
9. "what's actually draining your focus? (30-sec quiz)"
10. "take this quiz to find your burnout type"

## Formats

### A — "Notes app" screenshot (no AI)
Looks like a private 3am brain-dump. Highest "feels organic" score.
- Visual: iPhone Notes app screenshot/template, timestamp like "2:47 AM" at top.
- Best hooks: #1–4, #7, #8 (confessional).

### B — Fake tweet/X screenshot (no AI)
Reframe lines work well here — "wait, this is so real" reaction drives shares.
- Visual: tweet template, generic low-follower avatar, modest realistic engagement numbers.
- Best hooks: #5, #6, #7.

### C — POV photo + text overlay (AI-generated)
Candid, slightly imperfect "iPhone photo" aesthetic — avoid polished stock look.

**Prompt 1 — desk burnout:**
> Vertical iPhone photo, late at night, laptop open with too many browser tabs, half-empty coffee mug, hand resting over face in exhaustion, warm desk lamp glow, candid amateur smartphone photography, slightly overexposed, no text, no logos

**Prompt 2 — can't sleep:**
> Vertical iPhone photo, person lying in bed at night staring at the ceiling, phone face-down on the blanket, dim blue nightlight, tired but wide awake expression, candid low-fi smartphone photo style, grainy, no text

**Prompt 3 — daytime drain:**
> Vertical iPhone photo, person slumped on a couch in daylight still in pajamas at midday, phone screen glowing in their lap, messy living room softly lit, candid amateur photography, natural light, no text

### D — Reframe illustration (AI-generated, abstract)
Conceptual visual for "nervous system stuck in on-mode" — no people/app needed.

**Prompt 1 — stuck dial:**
> Minimalist flat vector illustration, a circular gauge or dial with the needle stuck deep in a red zone, soft muted color palette (cream background, one accent color), clean simple shapes, plenty of negative space, no text, no people

**Prompt 2 — open loop / closed loop:**
> Minimalist flat vector illustration, two circular arrow loops side by side — left one broken and incomplete in gray, right one fully connected and glowing in a calm green, clean modern vector style, soft cream background, no text

### E — Quiz teaser graphic (Canva, optional AI background)
Drives the click directly via progressive-commitment framing.
- Visual: clean card, short progress bar (~60–70% filled), checkmark/brain icon,
  headline #9 or #10, CTA "Take the quiz →".
- Background: plain color, or Format D Prompt 1 as a soft backdrop.
